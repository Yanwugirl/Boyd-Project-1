# Act I continue notification update

When one participant chose Continue together, the other participant previously received no visible invitation. After both participants confirmed, the completed view also retained Waiting for your partner in the header.

The other participant now sees a persistent dark-green banner with a direct confirmation button. The participant who initiated sees that their partner has been notified and can choose Keep chatting to cancel. The receiving browser tab title changes while a request is pending. After both confirm, completion labels replace the waiting state throughout both player views.

Validated with two live browser clients and the existing 10 passing automated tests. The Node server, model adapter and prompt are unchanged. Backup 1 remains unchanged.
